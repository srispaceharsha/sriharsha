Chapter One: Words of interest: Loquacious Obstinately Brusquely Interminable Stave Pontifically Obeisance Prostration Thoughts on characters: - Raju has suspense built into his character but not held tightly. Just enough information is given away at the right moment, leading the story backward and forward. I hadn't noticed this in my first reading. - Velan volunteers information. He is very helpful to get the story going! - Barber is absolutely believable character. I have seen similar one in life as such. Short lived character but very strong one. But - why did he say "no" the guessing game of how long Raju has been in the jail? Not clear.
